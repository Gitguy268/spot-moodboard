# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-23)

- First release: 40 pictures of Spot in three looks (Pinboard, Gallery, Cozy).
- Pinboard: drag the pictures around (press and hold on a phone), zoom out, tidy up; positions are kept per filter in your browser.
- Filters (Cozy, Festive, Studio, Art, Games), Shuffle, a lightbox with each picture's five main colours, and Spot's own colour swatches. Click any colour to copy it.
- Works from file:// by double-click, on phones, and with reduced motion.
